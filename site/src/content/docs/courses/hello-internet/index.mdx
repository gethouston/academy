**In the next hour you'll build a personal page by asking for it, and put it on the internet, where you can open it on your phone.** No code, no terminal.

## What you'll ship

*Your corner of the internet:* a simple page that says who you are, what you do, and one thing you want to build. It will live at a real address, something like `my-corner.vercel.app`, that anyone can open.

<CourseOutline />

## What you need

- A Mac or a Windows computer.
- A paid Claude plan (Pro or higher).
- An email address, to create a free account at Vercel, a hosting company.
- Your phone, to open the result.

## Done when

- Your page opens on your phone, at an address that starts with `https://`.
- You can find the page's folder on your computer.
- You can say in one sentence what "publishing" means.
