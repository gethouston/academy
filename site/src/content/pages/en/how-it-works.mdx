Houston Academy is a **program**: one path of courses, taken in order. Each course ends with something real that you ship. You don't need any technical background. You need an idea, a computer, and the patience to build one small piece at a time.

## How a lesson works

Every lesson has the same shape:

1. **Introduction:** what the lesson is about, and why it matters.
2. **Lesson overview:** what you'll learn.
3. **The ideas,** each explained with a picture you can step through.
4. **Assignment:** what to do. Usually that means asking Claude for something, with a prompt you can copy, and checking the result.
5. **Knowledge check:** a few questions to answer in your own words.

When you finish a lesson, click **Mark complete**. Your progress is saved in your browser, with no account needed.

## Your workbench

You work in the **Code** tab of the Claude desktop app: a chat, with a preview of what Claude builds and buttons for anything it wants to do. No terminal. You need a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher). Everything else in the program uses free tiers.

## Your tutor

In Course 0 you install a small add-on for Claude called **academy**. With it, the Claude you build with also acts as your teacher, inside your own project:

- after each important step, it adds a short note on what just happened, in plain words;
- `/academy:brief` helps you plan a slice before building it;
- `/academy:explain` explains the last thing Claude did, or any error;
- `/academy:check` checks whether you finished a course, by looking at your live project;
- it **stops Claude** before risky moves, like writing a secret key where anyone could see it.

## Watch out

Every lesson has at least one **"Houston, we have a problem"** box: a moment where something that looks fine is actually a problem. They come from real incidents, where real apps leaked data or ran up bills.

## The Builder's Loop

The method behind every project. You learn it in Course 2:

> **Idea → problem → smallest useful version → slices → for each slice: "done when" → Claude plans → you approve → Claude builds → you test → real people try it → you decide what's next.**
