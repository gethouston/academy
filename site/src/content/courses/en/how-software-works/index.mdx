You can't steer what you can't name. In this course, every word you'll hear while building software gets a plain explanation and a picture: client and server, frontend and backend, APIs, keys, databases, login, hosting, and AI models. It's the longest course in Level 1, on purpose. Along the way your Course 0 page gets live data from an API, and later a feature that needs a secret key, kept safe on a server.
