---
title: Think like a builder
description: "Course 2: plan like a product builder. Problems before solutions, the smallest useful version, and one slice at a time."
---

Most people open an AI agent and type their whole dream: *"Build me GTA 6, but multiplayer."* It never works, and the reason isn't the AI: **nobody can build or test everything at once.** This course teaches the method behind every project in the program, **the Builder's Loop**: problem → smallest useful version → slices → for each slice, a "done when" → Claude plans → you approve → Claude builds → you test → real people try it → you decide what's next. You'll pick your own product idea and ship its first slice.
