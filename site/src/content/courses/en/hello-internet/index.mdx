This is where it all begins! In one hour you'll build a personal page **by asking for it**, and put it on the internet, where you can open it on your phone. No code, no terminal. You'll need a Mac or Windows computer, a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher), an email address for a free Vercel account, and your phone.
