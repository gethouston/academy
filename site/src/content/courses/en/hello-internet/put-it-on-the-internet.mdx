## Introduction

Your page works, but only on your computer. In this lesson you'll see why, and put it on the internet, at a real address anyone can open.

## Lesson overview

This section contains a general overview of topics that you will learn in this lesson.

- Why nobody else can see your page yet.
- What publishing (deploying) means.
- What servers and hosting companies are.
- What should never go in a published folder.

## Two computers

Step through the picture:

<TwoComputers />

**Publishing**, also called **deploying**, means copying your files to a computer that never sleeps and is always connected to the internet. That computer is a **server**, and companies that rent them out are **hosting** companies. You'll use one called Vercel, which is free for projects like this.

When someone opens your address, their phone asks Vercel's computer for your file, and Vercel sends it. Your laptop isn't involved anymore: close it, and the page stays up.

## Everything in the folder becomes public

When you publish a folder, anyone can see everything in it. If you installed the tutor, you'll also see `CLAUDE.md` and a `.academy` folder. Those are notes for your tutor (your language, your course), with nothing private in them.

<RedFlag title="Publishing something private">
	Never publish a folder that has passwords, keys, personal documents or customer data in it. Later in the program, private things get a safe place of their own.
</RedFlag>

## Assignment

1. Ask Claude to check the folder first:

   <PromptCard>
   I'm about to publish this folder to the internet. Look at every file in it: is there anything I shouldn't make public? Don't change anything, just tell me.
   </PromptCard>

2. Go to [vercel.com/drop](https://vercel.com/drop) and create a free account (with your email or Google).
3. Drag your `my-corner` folder onto the page, choose a project name, and click **Deploy**.
4. Vercel gives you an address ending in `.vercel.app`. Open it, then open it on your phone, and send it to a friend.

If you change your page later, drag the folder again: it goes up as a new copy. In Course 4 you'll connect your project so changes publish on their own.
